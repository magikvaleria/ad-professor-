# Daily Practice — Faith Is Your Fortune

How to live the concepts, not just read them. Each card is a small daily
action; flip it to see which idea it comes from.

## Daily practice

> **Morning claim.** On waking, before you reach for your phone, assume the feeling of the wish fulfilled. Claim silently and feelingly: "I am that." Stay in it until it feels natural.
— Rising in consciousness · the claim

> **Drop the state.** When you catch yourself identified with a limitation, don't fight it. Withdraw your attention from it and rise to the state you would rather express.
— Rebirth

> **Guard your attention.** Through the day, notice where your attention goes. Whatever you attend to, you feed and keep alive. Take it off the problem; place it on the desired end.
— The vine and the branches

> **Enter the floating state.** Once a day, relax completely — deny the body, become faceless and formless, until you feel yourself floating. From that formless deep, feel that you already are the thing desired.
— The floating state

> **No masters.** When you feel dependent on something outside you to lift you, remember: only slaves have masters. Return the power to yourself — everything you need is within.
— The master is within

> **Don't look back.** When the old story tempts you, don't be Lot's wife. Keep your vision on the thing desired and keep walking in the claim "I am that."
— Leaving all and following

> **Evening check.** Before sleep, replay the day. Where did you react as the old self? Re-imagine one scene the way it would have gone had you been the person you are becoming.
— Rebirth · the claim
