# Concept → Application

The core ideas, made usable. Front: what it means. Flip it for how to apply
it and when to reach for it.

## Concept → Application

> **Consciousness is God.** Your own awareness of being — "I am" — is the only creative power. There is no cause outside of it.
Apply: Stop hunting for causes outside you. Whatever you want to change, change it first in your own consciousness by assuming you already are it.
When: Any time you start to blame circumstances, other people, or luck.

> **Rebirth.** You change your life not by improving the old self but by dropping it and rising to a new state of consciousness.
Apply: Name the state you're stuck in, consciously let it go, and occupy the new state as if it were already yours.
When: When you feel stuck, repeating the same patterns, or "working hard" with no change.

> **The claim — "I am that."** Expression follows the claim. You assume the feeling of already being it, and proof follows after.
Apply: Phrase the wish as a present-tense "I am…" and dwell in the feeling of it being already true. Don't ask; assume.
When: Setting an intention, or each morning before the day starts.

> **Attention is the sap of life.** Whatever you give attention to, you feed and keep alive; whatever you withdraw it from withers.
Apply: Audit what you keep replaying in your mind. Deliberately take attention off the unwanted and rest it on the desired end.
When: When you catch yourself rehearsing fears, grievances, or lack.

> **The floating state.** A relaxed, body-denying, receptive state is where impressions take hold most easily.
Apply: Relax until you feel formless and "floating," then, in that state, feel the wish fulfilled. Impress, don't strain.
When: Once a day, ideally drifting into sleep or just after waking.

> **The master is within.** No one outside you lifts you. The only everlasting master is your own consciousness.
Apply: When you lean on a person, mentor, or authority to "make it happen," return the power to yourself and make the claim within.
When: When you feel dependent, small, or like you need permission.

> **Giants and grasshoppers.** You can be to others only what you are to yourself. Raise your self-valuation and the "giants" shrink.
Apply: Before facing something intimidating, deliberately feel yourself to be the larger one — calm, capable, central.
When: Before a hard conversation, pitch, confrontation, or anything that feels bigger than you.

> **Don't look back.** Dwelling in the old story re-creates it. Keep your vision on the thing desired.
Apply: When the past pulls at you, refuse to replay it. Turn, like Lot, toward the promised land and keep walking in the claim.
When: When regret, nostalgia, or the old identity tries to reclaim you.

> **Expressions follow impressions.** The inner assumption comes first; the outer proof comes after — never before.
Apply: Don't wait for evidence to believe. Hold the assumption steady and let the world catch up to it.
When: When you're tempted to abandon the claim because "nothing has changed yet."
